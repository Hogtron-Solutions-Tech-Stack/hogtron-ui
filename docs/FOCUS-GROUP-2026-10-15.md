# HogTron UI focus group: 2026-10-15 (simulated)

Sean asked (2026-10-14 night): once the art was done, "create a focus group and have them look at this and see if
they understand ... the ease of use, quality of life things ... how intuitive it is, especially the menus ... then we
can build together a list ... to complete those before launch."

**What this is, honestly:** six *simulated* player personas walking the real addon. There were no live testers. Every
finding below points at something checkable: the options tree dumped from the real code (859 settings), the harness
renders in `docs/mockups/`, the slash list, or the defaults. It's useful for catching structure and discoverability
problems. It can't tell us how things *feel* in game; that needs real players (see "Next" at the end).

**What they looked at:**
- **Options window:** `art-options-painted.png` / `-flat.png`, plus the full options tree (`.scratch/options_tree.txt`, regenerated by `.scratch/dump_options.py`).
- **Quest dialog:** `dialog-detail-light.png`, `-complete-choice-light.png`, `-progress-light.png`, `-gossip-light.png`, `-detail-dark.png`.
- **Bars:** `cast-themes.png` (#34), `art-xpbar.png`, `art-infobar.png`.
- **Painted icons:** the menu bar and drawer set.
- **Defaults and commands:** `HogHeals/Core/Defaults.lua` and the 63 slash commands.

## The panel

| Persona | Who | Lens |
|---|---|---|
| **Kara** (34) | returning Classic player, casual healer, ~5 h/week, not technical | "can I get it looking right in 10 minutes" |
| **Dex** (22) | raider, lives in ElvUI + WeakAuras | density, profiles, performance, power-user control |
| **Marisol** (45) | plays with her son; UI scale 1.2, mild low vision | contrast, text size, icon legibility |
| **Tom** (58) | first time installing an addon (via CurseForge); has never typed a slash command | discoverability, first run |
| **Priya** (29) | UX designer, altoholic | information architecture, consistency |
| **Jonah** (40) | tank, deuteranopia (red-green colour blind) | anything that is colour-only |

## What each of them said

**Kara: "I like how it looks, but I don't know where to start."**
- **Too many places to start.** 11 sections and no search. To make her party frames bigger she opened General first (nothing there), then found Frames › Layout.
- **The quest window she never saw.** She loved the parchment quest window in the preview, then talked to three quest givers in her head-model of the game and saw nothing: auto-accept took the quest. "Where is the nice scroll?" *(Highest-impact finding of the whole session.)*
- **Two "Look" pages** (General › Look and Nameplates › Look): she changed the wrong one first.

**Dex: "Powerful. Now let me move my setup around."**
- **Profiles are hidden under Frames.** He assumed they only cover the party frames. Profiles should be top level, with import / export and per-character / per-spec.
- **Copy settings between action bars** (Bars has 167 settings, 10 bars × the same 15 knobs). This is already on the backlog (memory, 10/06).
- **Every painted layer must be switchable, and cheap.** He checked: Icons, Menus, the cast textures, `hud.xp.art`, and the info emblems all have switches. He wants **one** "Performance mode" toggle that turns off every animated layer: cast motes, scrolling textures, XP drift, object glow animation.
- **No search** in an 859-setting window is "a dealbreaker for a UI suite this size".

**Marisol: "Pretty. Some of it is small."**
- **Icon size.** The painted icons have more detail than the old glyphs. At the default 30 px menu bar she can't tell talents from the spellbook. She wants an icon size slider on the menu bar, or a "high contrast glyphs" choice. *(The "Flat glyphs" switch exists, but she wouldn't find it under General › Look.)*
- **The quest page is good.** Text size and width are adjustable, and the parchment contrast is fine.
- **Info bar text** is 11 px on a busy plaque. She wants the info bar's text size next to its "emblems" switch, which is where she looked.

**Tom: "How do I open it?"**
- **No way in that he could find.** He found no minimap button (it's off by default in General) and didn't know `/hh`. The small H beside the minimap is the drawer, but nothing tells him that.
- **The setup wizard only runs once.** If he closes it, the only way back is `/hh wizard`. He wants a **HogTron UI entry in the Esc game menu**.
- **"Needs a /reload" means nothing to him.** He wants a button that does it, which only exists in some places ("Apply (reload)").

**Priya: "Organise by what the player is doing, not by our module names."**
- **Sections are module names** (Frames, Units, Skin, HUD), not tasks. A player thinks "party & raid", "my character", "enemies", "questing", "map", "bars", "look & feel", "profiles".
- **Same names, different places:** General ×3, Look ×3, Layout ×2, Highlight ×2.
- **One topic split across sections:** XP bar, cast bars, icons (see the table above).
- **Page density:** Skin › Windows has 58 settings on one page. It needs subgroups or a "Show advanced" fold.
- **Controls don't match the look.** The new painted window frames flat cyan sliders and checkboxes. The controls should get the same brass and slate look.
- **Inconsistent wording:** some toggles are questions, some are nouns, some are sentences.

**Jonah: "Colour carries too much."**
- **Party frames:** green and red bars in the defaults. He relies on the health number, so he wants a colour-blind palette option.
- **Object glow:** herb green vs ore orange are near-identical to him. The name label (on by default) saves it. He'd like a small kind icon in front of the name.
- **Cast themes:** fine for him, because the spell name is on the bar.
- **XP vs rested** (cyan vs violet) reads fine.

## The list (merged, prioritised for launch)

Ordered by impact on a new player's first hour. Sizes: **S** = an evening, **M** = a session, **L** = several.

### P0: before launch
1. **First-run choice: "Read quests" vs "Fast questing".** Auto-accept on by default hides the new quest window. Ask once (in the setup wizard): read quests in the dialog (auto off), or fast (auto on, Shift to read). The quest dialog preview button goes in the same step. **S**
2. **A way in for people who don't type commands:**
   - a HogTron UI button in the Esc game menu;
   - the minimap button on by default;
   - the H drawer gets a first-run tooltip ("HogTron UI: click for settings").
   **S**
3. **Search in the options window:** a box over the sidebar that filters all 859 settings by name and description and jumps to the page, with the hit highlighted. **M**
4. **Profiles to the top level** (out of Frames), with import / export strings visible there. **S**
5. **"Reload now" button** wherever a setting needs a /reload, instead of telling the player to type it. **S**

### P1: soon after
6. **Task-based sections:** Party & Raid / Your character / Enemies & nameplates / Questing & map / Bars / Look & feel / Profiles. Rename the duplicate "General / Look / Layout / Highlight" pages, and put XP, cast bar and icon settings in one place each. **L** (pure re-plumbing of the options table; settings keep their keys, so nobody's saved settings move)
7. **Painted controls:** brass and slate checkboxes, sliders, dropdowns and buttons, so the painted window is consistent. OpenArt sheet + Panel.lua control makers. Credits are still in the cap. **M**
8. **Performance mode:** one toggle that turns off every animated / painted layer. **S**
9. **Icon size on the menu bar**, plus a "high-contrast glyphs" note next to it; info bar text size next to its emblems switch. **S**
10. **Split dense pages** (Skin › Windows 58, Frames › Indicators 46) into subgroups or a "Show advanced" fold. **M**

### P2: later
11. **Colour-blind palette** for party frames and object glow kinds; a small kind icon before the glow's name. **M**
12. **Copy settings from another action bar.** **S**
13. **Per-character / per-spec profile switching.** **M**
14. **Consistent setting wording:** a pass over all 859 labels (one style: short noun phrase + description). **M**

## What would make this real

The simulation found structure problems. It can't judge feel, in-game legibility at real UI scales, or what testers actually miss:
- **Short live sessions.** Two or three real players (the beta.2 testers on CurseForge are the obvious pool), 20 minutes each. Give them a task list: "make your party frames bigger", "turn off auto-accept", "find your profile", "make the info bar show coordinates". Note where they get stuck.
- **A light usage diag.** Have `/hh` record which pages get opened, so we can see which pages nobody ever finds. This is local and opt-in, and nothing is sent anywhere.
